The Appearance dialog (`openAppearanceDialog` from `windows/appearance`) shows one small window per scheme: Standard, Skyline and High Contrast. Each picture wears its own scheme whatever scheme the page wears. OK hands the pick to the caller, and Cancel, the ✕ and Esc leave the look as it was. The schemes themselves, and the one attribute that dresses a page in one, are `windows/schemes`.

Press OK here to dress this site in the picked scheme. The Options menu shows the same pick.
