Every reusable piece of `@verticopolis/components`, the renderer, windows,
menus and audio of [Verticopolis](https://verticopolis.com), drawn from the
package's own stylesheets, painters and synthesizer. Each page loads the built
package the way a game would.

Pick a color scheme from the **Options** menu to see the controls in it, and
Light, Dark or Match My Device for the page around them.

- **Windows and controls:** [buttons](controls/buttons/), [windows](controls/windows/), [wells, fields and key-value rows](controls/wells/), a [sample menu bar](controls/menu-bar/), [message boxes](controls/message-boxes/) and the [icons](controls/icons/).
- **Desktop:** [the desk](desktop/desk/), live with its windows, window menu and a sample taskbar, and [where its windows open](desktop/layout/) on three screen sizes.
- **[Sounds](sounds/):** every action effect, both music tracks and the ambience of each part of a tower.
- **The tower:** [lobbies](tower/lobbies/), [stairs and escalators](tower/stairs-and-escalators/), [elevators](tower/elevators/), every [room](tower/rooms/) and all the [people](tower/people/).
- **Docs:** the [architecture](docs/architecture/), the [design system](docs/design-system/), [the desk](docs/desk/), the [audio layer](docs/audio/) and the [sprite atlas](docs/atlas/), from the repository's own Markdown.
