Every reusable piece of `@verticopolis/components`, the renderer, windows,
menus and audio of [Verticopolis](https://verticopolis.com), drawn from the
package's own stylesheets, painters and synthesizer. Each page loads the built
package the way a game would.

Pick a color scheme from the **Options** menu to see the controls in it, and
Light, Dark or Match My Device for the page around them.

- **Windows and controls:** [buttons](controls/buttons/), [windows](controls/windows/), [wells, fields and key-value rows](controls/wells/), a [sample menu bar](controls/menu-bar/) and the [icons](controls/icons/).
- **[Sounds](sounds/):** every action effect, both music tracks and the ambience of each part of a tower.
- **The tower:** [lobbies](tower/lobbies/), [stairs and escalators](tower/stairs-and-escalators/), [elevators](tower/elevators/) and every [room](tower/rooms/).
