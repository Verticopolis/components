The Control Panel is the window a 1995 player opened for their settings: a row of large icons on a white well, each named under it, with an object count along the foot. It comes from `windows/desk/deskControlPanel` and the desk's stylesheet, `styles/game/desk.css`.

- The game picks the icons and their order from the four the package draws (Display, Appearance, Sounds and Settings), names each one and gives it a tooltip.
- A press on an icon goes to the game, which opens whatever that setting is. The window runs nothing of its own.
- The game can change an icon's name while the window is open, as the game's Sounds icon reads "Sounds: On" or "Sounds: Off".
- The ✕ closes the window, and the game opens it again from its own menu or tray.
- The title screen can hold one too (`DeskTitle`'s `controlPanel`): it opens in the middle of the title window, or across it under the menu bar on a phone, and stays where it opens.

Here each icon only says it was pressed, and Sounds flips its name.
