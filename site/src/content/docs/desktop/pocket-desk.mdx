A phone, held either way, and a smaller upright tablet get the pocket desk in place of [the desk](../desk/): the tower window fills the screen and never moves, with the Information bar, the tower's view and Tools inside it, and the taskbar along the bottom. It comes from `windows/desk/deskPocket`, with the desk's windows and taskbar, and its stylesheet is `styles/game/desk-pocket.css`.

- `deskMode` decides which desk a screen gets: the full desk on a computer or a touchscreen at least 1024 by 600, the pocket desk on a smaller touchscreen, and none otherwise.
- The game puts `desk`, `desk-touch` and `desk-pocket` on the body, draws the grid with `pocketBody` inside the tower window, and moves its own Information bar, Tools and tower view into it, in that order. The stylesheet places them by the game's element ids (`#topbar`, `#palette`, `#stage`).
- On an upright phone the Information bar takes two rows, the tower's view fills the middle, and Tools is a row of category tabs over a strip of tools at the bottom. Sideways, Tools moves to the left of the view.
- The taskbar and the menu rows are 44 pixels tall, and each title-bar button has a 44 pixel touch area.
- The Bulletin, Tower Info and the Control Panel open across the top of the desk, one at a time, with the tower showing below. The Bulletin stops at a third of the desk, and a drag by the title bar slides any of them out of the way. Here the tower's Window menu, the Start menu and the taskbar open the Bulletin.

Here the frame is an upright phone's screen, over the game's whole stylesheet, with a sample tower, the sample Information bar and sample tools. The rules are in [the desk doc](../../docs/desk/#the-pocket-desk).
