On a computer the game runs as a 1994 desktop: each part of the game is its own window on a teal desk, with a taskbar along the bottom. This sample desk uses the package's window manager (`windows/desk/deskManager`), desk windows (`windows/desk/deskWindow`), window menu (`windows/desk/deskWindowMenu`) and taskbar (`windows/desk/deskTaskbar`) with the desk's stylesheet.

- Drag a window by its title bar. A press anywhere on a window brings it to the front and lights its title bar.
- Minimize sends a window to the taskbar; press its taskbar button to bring it back, or press the front window's button to minimize it.
- The tower window maximizes with its button or a double-click on its title bar (the small windows stay on top of it and step below its menu bar, and step back on restore), and resizes by the grip at its bottom right corner: a dotted outline follows the pointer and the window takes the size on release. Tools resizes in height only.
- Right-click a title bar or a taskbar button (or press Shift+F10 on a taskbar button) for the window menu: Restore, Minimize, Maximize and Close, each grayed when it does not apply.
- Close the tower or the Bulletin with its ✕ and open it again from the Verticopolis menu, which Up and Down move through and Escape closes. Info and Tools never close. Arrange Windows puts every window back.
- The speaker in the tray turns the sound on and off (here it only changes its look), and the clock holds at 9:15 AM so the screenshots hold still.

A game names the Verticopolis menu's items and decides what each one does. On a smaller touchscreen the game gets the pocket desk instead, described in the doc. The desk is fixed at 720 by 460 pixels, so a narrow screen scrolls it. The rules are in [the desk doc](../../docs/desk/).
